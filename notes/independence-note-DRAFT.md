Note on independence. This design was developed in 1998 as
proprietary industry work, without access to the published
literature, and was reconstructed from the author's memory in 2026,
after the copyright in the original work had lapsed. Parts of it
therefore duplicate published results independently: non-binary buddy
variants (Peterson and Norman, 1977), aggregate-steered tree
allocation (Brent, 1989), search-free placement at the price of
incomplete memory use (Ogasawara, 1995; Masmano et al., 2004, 2008),
and buddy allocation in hardware (Agun and Chang, 2003); see also the
survey of Wilson et al. (1995). The duplication is independent in
both directions: the author had seen none of the earlier work, and
the 1998 design predates the closest later work. Similarities to the
works cited are independent duplication, not derivation.
